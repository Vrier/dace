How this works

The cells are the Judge's 22,400, in a fixed random order, taken in blocks of 30. Each block gets three passes; in every pass the 30 cells are split into three requests of ten, grouped differently each time, so a cell's three answers come from three different contexts. The first answer counts as the LLM judge's judgement, the other two as repeats, which consolidation reports as its stability. The prompt shows each card as a person sees it: the question, the minimal pair, the sentence and the buttons.

Requests use the most capable model tier and take up to a minute or two each. A stopped or failed run picks up where it left off next time.
